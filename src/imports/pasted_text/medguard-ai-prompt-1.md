---

Figma Prompt

Product

Design a modern healthcare web application called MedGuard AI.

Tagline: AI-Powered Medication Compatibility & Drug Interaction Checker

The application helps users safely manage their medications by detecting drug-drug interactions, predicting risk levels using machine learning, providing compatibility scores, explaining interactions in simple language, suggesting safer alternatives, tracking medication history, and acting as an intelligent healthcare assistant.

The design should feel like a premium healthcare platform similar to Notion + Apple Health + Headspace + Stripe Dashboard + Linear, with clean spacing, soft glassmorphism, subtle gradients, rounded cards, beautiful illustrations, smooth micro-interactions, and accessibility-focused UI.

Avoid outdated hospital-style interfaces. The experience should feel calming, intelligent, trustworthy, and futuristic.
